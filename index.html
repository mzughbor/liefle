FAQ

Good questions.

How does the free 24-hour subdomain work?

You get a yourname.liefle.app URL for 24 hours to test the editor and templates. After that we delete it unless you upgrade to a paid plan.

How do I prove I own my domain?

We give you a single NS or TXT record to add at your registrar. Click Verify and we confirm in seconds.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard. Your site stays live until the end of the billing period.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The wizard does the work. If you do code, you can drop into HTML/CSS on the Pro plan.
